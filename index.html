    Tips for an accurate height
    • The stick needs to be right against the trunk. If it's closer to the camera than the tree, the height comes out too low; if it's farther away, too high.
    • Shoot from well back, ideally at least as far from the tree as it is tall, and hold the camera level.
    • On wide, rounded crowns the highest point you can see is often in front of the true top, so heights tend to read low.
    • When you measure several trees in one photo, each tree needs to be about as far from the camera as the stick is. Trees farther back come out too short.
    • Zoom in before placing or adjusting points. The ± figure only reflects how precisely the points are placed, not the setup issues above.
